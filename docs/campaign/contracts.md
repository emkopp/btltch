# Contracts

Employment terms that drive everything else. A BattleTech mercenary contract (per Campaign
Operations / Field Manual: Mercenaries) is defined by its mission, length, pay, and — critically —
its **transport, salvage, command, and support rights**.

## Terms glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Mission type** | Garrison, Cadre, Security, Recon, Riot Duty, Guerrilla, Pirate Hunting, Objective Raid, Extraction, Assault, Diversionary, Planetary Assault, Relief. Each has a base pay multiplier. |
| **Command rights** | Independent → House → Liaison → Integrated. More employer control = less pay flexibility. |
| **Salvage rights** | % of recovered material the unit keeps (Exchange / Independent / none). |
| **Support** | Straight Support vs Battle Loss Compensation (employer replaces losses). |
| **Transport** | Who ships the unit in-system and between systems, and who pays. |
