# BattleTech Design Quirks — Master Reference

**Design Quirks** are an *optional* BattleTech rules set (introduced in *Strategic Operations*,
expanded and collected in the *BattleMech Manual*, with further items in *Interstellar Operations*,
the *Alpha Strike Companion*, and the *Record Sheets: 3145 New Tech, New Upgrades*). They give an
individual unit design special positive or negative traits that normal construction rules don't
capture.

Key facts:

- Each quirk has a **point value** — positive quirks *cost* points, negative quirks *refund*
  (rebate) points. A "balanced" design is meant to offset positives with equal-or-greater negatives.
- Quirks are **not** reflected in Battle Value, so both players must agree to use them.
- Rules differ between **BattleTech (BT)**, **Alpha Strike (AS)**, and **Strategic BattleForce
  (SBF)**. The default text is the standard **BattleTech** effect.
- Some quirks apply to a single weapon/bay and their cost must be paid per weapon.

### Alpha Strike / SBF designators

Where a quirk behaves differently outside of standard BattleTech, the ruleset-specific portion is
tagged inline so you can see exactly which sentence applies only to that game:

- **`⟦AS⟧`** — the following text applies **only in Alpha Strike**.
- **`⟦AS/SBF⟧`** — applies **only in Alpha Strike and Strategic BattleForce**.
- **`⟦SBF⟧`** — applies **only in Strategic BattleForce**.

Any effect text *without* a tag is the standard BattleTech rule. The **AS?** column flags at a
glance whether a quirk carries any Alpha Strike (or SBF) specific rule.

> Source: *List of Design Quirks*, Sarna.net (compiled from *Strategic Operations*, *BattleMech
> Manual*, *Alpha Strike Companion*, *Interstellar Operations*, *Record Sheets: 3145 NTNU*).
> Quirks are an optional rule.

## Positive Design Quirks

| Quirk | Pts | AS? | Effect |
|-------|----:|:---:|--------|
| **Accurate Weapon** | Var. | ✔ | Marked weapon/bay gets **−1 to-hit**. Cost = 1 per 5 pts (or fraction) of max damage; 2 for 0-damage weapons (e.g. TAG). `⟦AS/SBF⟧` deals 1 dmg per level if the attack misses by only 1. |
| **Anti-Aircraft Targeting** | Var. | ✔ | **−1 to-hit vs airborne units** (VTOL, WiGE, fighters, DropShips, combat drops) while the unit is on the ground. Cost = 1 per 7 pts of max weapon damage. `⟦AS⟧` stacks with AA Specialists SPA. `⟦SBF⟧` penalizes units targeting Ground units. |
| **Atmospheric Flyer** | 3 | — | Bonus to control rolls when flying inside atmosphere. Aerospace fighters, DropShips, Small Craft. |
| **Barrel Fist** | 1 | — | A handless 'Mech may **punch without the usual +1 penalty**. Requires a Lower Arm actuator; paid per fist. |
| **Battle Computer** | 5 | ✔ | Grants an **Initiative bonus** to the whole force (needs a conscious pilot). Not cumulative with Command BattleMech. `⟦AS⟧` applies only with 4+ unit formations and adds 2 to the force's MHQ ability. `⟦SBF⟧` grants Leadership bonus and MHQ:6. |
| **Battlefists** | 2 | ✔ | Modified hand actuators give a **bonus to hit with Punch** attacks. Requires Hand actuators. `⟦AS⟧` applies to all normal physical attacks. |
| **Combat Computer** | 3 | ✔ | **Total heat generated each turn is reduced by 4** (min 0). `⟦AS⟧` reduces Overheat by 1 each turn (units with an Overheat value only). |
| **Command BattleMech** | 2 | ✔ | Small **Initiative bonus** to the force (conscious pilot). Not cumulative with Battle Computer. `⟦AS⟧` adds 1 each to MHQ and Initiative in a 4+ unit formation (no stack). `⟦SBF⟧` adds MHQ:3. |
| **Compact 'Mech** | 1 | — | Takes **half the normal 'Mech-bay space** in transport (still a full Repair bay). Light/Medium with Narrow/Low Profile (BT). |
| **Cowl** | 4 | ✔ | **+3 armor vs side and rear head hits** (not front). `⟦AS⟧` instead allows the unit to ignore one "Unit Destroyed" critical hit. |
| **Directional Torso Mount** | 2–3 | — | Mount fires into **either the front or the rear arc** (facing set at start, changed like a torso twist, doesn't reset). 9+ on a location hit destroys it. 3-pt version (quads) = full 360° turret. |
| **Distracting** | 1 | ✔ | Imposes a **penalty to enemy morale checks** (Tactical Morale rules); cumulative with Demoralizer SPA. Doesn't affect Drones. `⟦SBF⟧` penalizes morale rolls of any enemy Unit in the same hex. |
| **Docking Arms** | 1 | — | Bonus to piloting rolls for DropShips/Small Craft docking with this unit. JumpShips, Space Stations, WarShips. |
| **Easy to Maintain** | 1 | — | **Bonus to repair and replacement rolls** — easier to service by design. |
| **Easy to Pilot** | 2 | ✔ | **Bonus to most Piloting Skill Rolls** when piloted by sub-Elite crews (training units). `⟦AS⟧` removes the Elite restriction. |
| **Extended Torso Twist** | 3 | ✔ | May **torso-twist one extra hexside** (up to two each way); can grant twisting to quads. `⟦AS⟧` instead grants a full **360° arc of fire** (keeps rear arc for damage). |
| **Fast Reload** | 1 | — | **Halves reload time** (whole 'Mech, a location, or a weapon — cost unchanged). |
| **Fine Manipulators** | 0 | — | May mount vehicle-style manipulators in torso/arm locations at **no weight cost**. |
| **Good Reputation** | 0 | — | Raises resale worth by **+10% per level**. Not for Clan 'Mechs. |
| **Hyper-Extending Actuators** | 1 | ✔ | May **flip arms into the rear arc** even with lower-arm/hand actuators. `⟦AS⟧` may make an attack of 1/3 standard value (round down) into the rear arc (minimum-damage eligible). |
| **Improved Cooling Jacket** | 1 | — | Marked weapon/bay generates **−1 heat** when fired (never to 0); per-weapon cost. |
| **Improved Communications** | 1 | — | **Ignores the first level of ECM Sensor Ghosts**; with a Satellite Uplink, can uplink without movement/attack penalty. |
| **Improved Life Support** | 1 | — | **Reduces apparent heat** for pilot-damage checks after a Life Support crit; **doubles** life-support endurance. |
| **Improved Sensors** | 3 | — | Counts as an **Active Probe** (4 hexes IS / 5 Clan) even without one; +2 hexes if also equipped with a real probe. |
| **Improved Targeting (Short / Medium / Long)** | 3–5 | — | **Bonus to-hit in the chosen range bracket** (one purchase per bracket). Incompatible with Variable-Range Targeting; not for physical combat. |
| **Internal Bomb Bay** | 3 | — | May use internal cargo space as a **bomb bay** (risky in BT — ground fire can detonate ordnance). Aero, DropShips, Small Craft. |
| **Jettison-Capable Weapon** | 1/wpn | — | Weapon can be **ejected and later reattached** (like an ammo reload). Up to two such weapons; can't be used on OmniPod/handheld weapons. |
| **Modular Weapons** | 1 | ✔ | **Half the usual time** to swap weapons/install customizations (not in-place repairs). Redundant on Omnis. `⟦AS⟧` gives a bonus to Repair Rolls and reduces repair times as though the unit had the OMNI special. |
| **Multi-Trac** | 2 | ✔ | May **attack multiple targets in the front/arm arcs without the secondary-target penalty** (rear arc still penalized). SL Advanced Neurohelmets grant this. `⟦AS⟧` instead makes two half-damage attacks at separate units (cumulative with Multi-Tasking SPA; minimum-damage rules apply). |
| **Narrow / Low Profile** | 3 | ✔ | **+1 penalty to enemy ranged to-hit** rolls (not physical). `⟦AS⟧` adds a +1 modifier to attacks against it (not physical). |
| **Nimble Jumper** | 3 | — | When jumping, may **deviate up to one hex** from the direct path; attacks against it get +1 TMM (total +2 for jumping), but its own jumping attacks take +4 instead of +3. Light/Medium with Jump MP ≥ Walk MP. |
| **Overhead Arms** | 2 | ✔ | Arm weapons fire from an **effectively higher level**, potentially negating a target's Partial Cover. `⟦AS⟧` instead: a unit behind cover of equal height may ignore 2" of blocking terrain; damage halved, +2 penalty (AS Companion version differs from current BT). |
| **Protected Actuators** | 1 | ✔ | **Penalty to Battle Armor / infantry Leg and Swarm attacks** against this 'Mech. `⟦AS⟧` adds a +1 penalty to infantry making an anti-'Mech attack. |
| **Power Reverse** | 3 | — | Combat vehicle may use **Flank MP moving in reverse**. Wheeled/Tracked only. |
| **Reinforced Legs** | 1 | ✔ | Takes only **half self-damage from a successful Death From Above**. `⟦AS⟧` instead reduces DFA self-damage by 1 point. |
| **Rugged** | 1–2 | — | Longer between required maintenance cycles: **1 pt = twice as long, 2 pts = three times** as long. |
| **Rumble Seat** | 0 | — | A **simple second seat** (passenger/observer/instructor); can trigger emergency shutdown. Not on Light/ultralight, small/torso/interface cockpits. Passenger shares pilot's hit/heat risk. |
| **Searchlight** | 1 | — | Fitted with a **high-powered searchlight** (can be destroyed by damage from some directions). |
| **Stabilized Weapon** | 1/7dmg | — | Marked weapon **reduces the unit's own running/sprinting to-hit penalty by 1**; per-weapon cost. |
| **Stable** | 2 | — | **Bonus to Piloting Skill Rolls forced by physical attacks**. BattleMechs only. |
| **Trailer Hitch** | 1 | — | May **tow a second unit** that also has the quirk. Tracked/Wheeled combat vehicles only. |
| **Ubiquitous** | 1 | — | Mass-produced for centuries → **replacement parts are easy to find**. Applies to everything. |
| **Variable Range Targeting** | 1/5dmg | ✔ | Each End Phase, choose short or long: next turn gets **−1 to-hit at the chosen range but +1 at the opposite**. Incompatible with Improved Targeting. `⟦AS/SBF⟧` instead applies a flat −1 bonus to all attacks unless they are being split. |
| **Vestigial Hands** | 0 | — | A handless 'Mech (no arm crit slots) may **lift/drop scenario-objective items**. |
| **VTOL Rotor Arrangement** | 1 | — | VTOL has **dual or coaxial rotors** (advanced vehicle movement rules): trades maneuverability/turn-mode characteristics. VTOLs only. |

## Negative Design Quirks

(Point values are **rebates** — points the design gets back.)

| Quirk | Rebate | AS? | Effect |
|-------|-------:|:---:|--------|
| **Ammunition Feed Problem** | 1 | ✔ | A ballistic/missile weapon is **prone to jamming** (or, in extreme cases, exploding): extra roll after each to-hit. `⟦AS⟧` restricted to ammo-dependent weapons; each time they fire, pass an extra 2d6 roll or the attack is cut by half its ammo-dependent value (minimum damage does not apply). |
| **Atmospheric Flight Instability** | 2 | — | Aerospace unit is **unstable in atmosphere** — penalty to control rolls. Aero, DropShips, Small Craft. |
| **Bad Reputation** | 1 | — | Sound but unpopular — **worth only half normal resale value** (players still pay full to buy). Clan variant gives an automatic dezgra point. |
| **Cooling System Flaws** | 3 | ✔ | **Generates extra heat** after falling, taking a damage-forced PSR, or making/receiving a physical attack (roll to avoid, permanent for the game). `⟦AS⟧` the roll applies only if the unit takes a physical attack or more than 2 points of damage from a single attack. |
| **Cramped Cockpit** | 2 | ✔ | Suffers the **same penalties as a Small Cockpit** but uses a standard cockpit's weight/slots (downside, no savings). `⟦AS⟧` applies a +1 penalty to Control Roll and Physical Attack rolls. |
| **Difficult Ejection** | 1 | ✔ | Pilot forced to eject must make a **PSR or take extra damage** during ejection. `⟦AS⟧` if the roll is failed, the pilot is automatically killed. |
| **Difficult to Maintain** | 1 | — | **Penalty to repair, salvage, and maintenance** target numbers. (HarJel and RISC supercooled myomer force this.) |
| **EM Interference** | 1 | ✔ | An energy weapon with poor shielding **disables the unit's electronics** (probes, Artemis, C3, NARC, MASC, ECM, stealth armor, Streaks, etc.) in the round after it fires. `⟦AS⟧` counts as inside a hostile ECM field each turn it makes any attack not exclusively using ART-X/AC/FLK/IF/LRM/SRM/TOR. `⟦SBF⟧` assigns a Detection-Phase penalty. |
| **Exposed Actuators** | 1 | — | Poorly-armored actuators make the 'Mech an **easier target for Leg and Swarm attacks**. |
| **Exposed Weapon Linkage** | 2 | ✔ | A designated weapon type is **prone to critical hits** when its location is struck. `⟦AS⟧` extra roll each time the unit takes damage; if failed, suffer a Weapon Critical Hit in addition to other effects. |
| **Fragile Fuel Tank** | 2 | ✔ | Aerospace/vehicle fuel tank has a **greater chance of exploding** on a critical hit. `⟦AS⟧` extra roll each time the unit takes damage; if failed, the unit is automatically destroyed. |
| **Gas Hog** | 2 | ✔ | Inefficient engine **burns double fuel** above cruising/safe thrust. Not for fusion/fission ground/naval units. `⟦AS⟧` only affects Aerospace units using Capital-scale movement; extra fuel by map zone. |
| **Hard to Pilot** | 2 | ✔ | **Penalty to all Piloting/Driving Skill Rolls** (inherently difficult). Auto-applied to DNI units without implants and "cyber-haunted" neurohelmets. `⟦AS⟧` the modifier applies to Control Rolls and all Physical Attacks. |
| **Illegal Design** | 0 | ✔ | Allows breaking construction rules but **penalizes repair/modification**; failed repair adds a Nonfunctional quirk. Must also take Obsolete. `⟦AS⟧` doubled Obsolete penalties, auto-destroyed on a Repair roll of "2", never replaceable, 5× cost. |
| **Inaccurate Weapon** | 1/5dmg | ✔ | Marked weapon/bay gets **+1 to-hit**. `⟦AS/SBF⟧` if it hits with MoS ≤ 1, reduce damage by 1 per level of the quirk (apply before Glancing Blows). |
| **Large DropShip** | 1 | — | So large it **occupies two docking collars** instead of one. DropShips only. |
| **Low-Mounted Arms** | 2 | ✔ | Arm weapons fire from **one level up instead of two**; arms are covered by terrain/water as if they were legs. Incompatible with Overhead Arms. `⟦AS⟧` damage is halved if firing across blocking terrain taller than the unit's own height (round down, min 1). |
| **No / Minimal Arms** | 2 | ✔ | **Penalty to stand-up PSRs**; **cannot make physical attacks or carry items** with arms. `⟦AS⟧` units without arms cannot use External Cargo or Climbing rules. |
| **No Cooling Jacket** | 2 | — | Marked weapon **generates extra heat** when fired (more than Poor Cooling Jacket). |
| **No Ejection Mechanism** | 2 | — | **No escape system** — pilot can't eject in an emergency. Aero, BattleMechs, Small Craft. |
| **Nonfunctional Item** | 5 | — | Permanently **disables one component/system** — never repairable. Can be taken multiple times. Any unit. |
| **Non-Standard Parts** | 1 | — | Uses uncommon parts → **penalty to obtain/locate replacements**. |
| **No Torso Twist** | 2 | ✔ | Cannot perform the torso-twist maneuver. **(Removed by BattleMech Manual Errata v6.0.)** `⟦AS⟧` the rear arc stays the same for damage, but for firing the unit uses a restricted forward arc. |
| **Obsolete** | 4 | ✔ | Tied to an out-of-production date; **increasingly hard to repair/find parts** and resale drops over time. Returning to production takes a decade to clear. Any unit. `⟦AS⟧` flat +2 to all repair/salvage rolls, can't be replaced with an identical unit, 3× cost. |
| **Oversized** | 3 | ✔ | Larger than normal → **easier to hit**, harder to use terrain/cover, terrain-movement damage. Heavy/Assault 'Mechs & vehicles. Incompatible with Narrow/Low Profile. (BattleTech and Alpha Strike share the same effect.) `⟦SBF⟧` a flat to-hit bonus for enemies, with no other penalties. |
| **Poor Cooling Jacket** | 1 | — | Marked weapon **generates extra heat** (less than No Cooling Jacket). |
| **Poor Life Support** | 1 | ✔ | **Worsens heat-level modifiers** for pilot damage and **halves** life-support operating time. `⟦AS⟧` if the unit ends its turn carrying heat, roll or the crew becomes Stunned. |
| **Poor Performance** | 3 | ✔ | Can't accelerate quickly — must spend **one turn at cruising/walking** before using full movement next turn. `⟦AS/SBF⟧` reduce TMM by 1 (in SBF, only if more than half the formation has the quirk). |
| **Poor Sealing** | 2 | ✔ | Susceptible to **leaks/hull breaches/flooding** — extra checks in hostile environments, including on first entry. `⟦SBF⟧` only the breach-roll penalty applies. |
| **Poor Targeting (Short / Medium / Long)** | 2–4 | — | **Penalty to to-hit in the affected range bracket** (up to all three brackets). |
| **Poor Workmanship** | 1 | ✔ | Equipment is **one quality step lower**; bonus to enemy critical-damage rolls. `⟦AS/SBF⟧` reroll the first "no effect" critical result (in SBF, only if at least half the formation has the quirk). |
| **Prototype** | 2 | — | Immature tech → **significantly increased chance of critical hits**. |
| **Ramshackle** | 3 | — | Shoddy maintenance — a **random non-physical negative quirk** (e.g. EM Interference) is applied each battle until it ends. BattleMechs/IndustrialMechs. |
| **Sensor Ghosts** | 2 | ✔ | Sensors generate ghost images → **severe targeting penalties** when shooting. A malfunctioning SL Advanced Neurohelmet can cause this. (BattleTech and Alpha Strike share the effect.) `⟦SBF⟧` causes to-hit and Detection-Phase penalties if more than half the formation has the quirk. |
| **Slow Traverse** | 1 | — | 'Mech turret **rotates only one hex per turn** instead of freely. |
| **Static Ammo Feed** | 1 | ✔ | Crude feed **prevents swapping ammo types** in combat — pick one type before the game (per weapon type in BT). `⟦AS⟧` all weapons lose the ability to choose ammo types. Often paired with Exposed Ammo Feed. |
| **Unbalanced** | 1 | — | Inherently off-balance → **penalty to certain Piloting Skill Rolls**. BattleMechs only. |
| **Un-streamlined** | 1 | ✔ | **Cannot enter/operate in atmosphere**; if it does, treated as a JumpShip (BT). `⟦AS⟧` such a unit is destroyed instead. Aero, DropShips, Small Craft. |
| **Weak Head Armor** | 1/pt | — | Head armor is **treated as a number of points lower** than recorded. Rebate = 1 per lost point. BattleMechs only. |
| **Weak Legs** | 1 | ✔ | Not built for physical stress — **chance of a leg critical when kicked or after a DFA**, even without armor breach. `⟦AS⟧` roll or suffer an MP critical hit after every DFA attempt. |
| **Weak Undercarriage** | 1 | ✔ | Flimsy landing gear — **collapses and takes damage** on a bad-margin landing PSR. `⟦AS⟧` the margin of failure needed is only 0; damage is based on the unit's Size and it cannot lift off until repaired. Aero, DropShips, Small Craft. |

## Notes on balance

- *Strategic Operations* only **encourages** designers to offset positive quirks with negatives of
  equal or greater value; some canon designs don't balance out.
- **Strategic BattleForce** and **Alpha Strike** now **require** player-built units to balance quirk
  points, though several quirks are removed from those rulesets (SBF has the fewest, BT the most).
- Quirks lack a conventional balancing mechanism (they're not in Battle Value), so they can
  introduce imbalance in one-off games — hence the mutual-consent rule.
