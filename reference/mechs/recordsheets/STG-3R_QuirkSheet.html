What are Design Quirks?

Design Quirks are an optional BattleTech rules set (from Strategic Operations, collected in the BattleMech Manual) that give an individual 'Mech special positive or negative traits normal construction rules don't capture. Each has a point value — positives cost points, negatives refund them — and they're not counted in Battle Value, so both players must agree to use them. The Stinger is a tough, everywhere-you-look scout with one notorious flaw: a miserably tight cockpit.

Positive Quirks

Rugged (1) Positive · 1 pt

What is it?
The chassis is built tough and forgiving, tolerating longer stretches without a full maintenance cycle.
Game effect
At 1 point, the 'Mech can go twice as long between required maintenance cycles before it starts to degrade. (At 2 points it would be three times as long — the Stinger has the 1-point version.)
Ideal for a scout that operates far from proper repair bays for extended patrols. The Stinger keeps soldiering on with minimal attention, which helped it survive the Succession Wars in huge numbers.

Ubiquitous Positive · 1 pt

What is it?
Mass-produced for centuries by many factories, so spare parts are common everywhere.
Game effect
Easier to find replacement parts. Rolls to locate/obtain spares are simpler because the chassis is so widespread.
The Stinger is the second-most produced 'Mech in the Inner Sphere (right behind its rival, the Wasp) — over 200,000 built. Parts are almost never a problem.
Negative Quirks

Cramped Cockpit Negative · −2 pts

What is it?
The cockpit is uncomfortably tight — an average-sized MechWarrior has to physically squeeze in and often needs help getting out. The controls are awkward to work under stress.
BattleTech effect
The 'Mech suffers the same penalties as if it mounted a Small Cockpit (a piloting/gunnery penalty), even though it actually uses a normal cockpit's weight and critical slots — so it gets the downside with none of the weight savings.
Alpha Strike
Applies a +1 penalty to Control Rolls and Physical Attack rolls.
This is the Stinger's signature drawback — a running joke among its pilots. It's often cited as the reason later variants (like the STG-5R) prioritized a roomier head assembly with a full-head ejection system.
Quirks per BattleMech Manual, p. 95 (Stinger entry). Rules summarized from Strategic Operations / BattleMech Manual and the Alpha Strike Companion via Sarna.net. Design Quirks are optional; both players must agree to use them.
