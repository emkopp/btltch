What are Design Quirks?

Design Quirks are an optional BattleTech rules set (first in Strategic Operations, fully collected in the BattleMech Manual). They give an individual 'Mech design special positive or negative traits that normal construction rules don't capture — the little things that make one chassis feel different from another on the battlefield. Each quirk has a point value; positive quirks cost points and negative quirks refund them, and a "balanced" design is meant to offset the two. Quirks are not counted in Battle Value, so both players must agree to use them.

Positive Quirks

Extended Torso Twist Positive · 3 pts

What is it?
The 'Mech's waist/torso is unusually flexible, letting it rotate its upper body farther to bring weapons to bear on targets to the side without moving its legs.
BattleTech effect
When it torso-twists, it may twist one extra hexside beyond the normal one-hexside limit (so up to two hexsides left or right). This widens its arc of fire each turn.
Alpha Strike
Gains a full 360° arc of fire (it still keeps a Rear arc for taking damage).
On the UrbanMech this is the famous "spinning trash can" trait — a slow, nearly immobile ambusher that compensates for its 2/3 movement by swiveling to shoot attackers coming from almost any direction without having to waddle around. (Note: MechWarrior Online's continuous 360° spin is an apocryphal exaggeration of this quirk.)

Narrow / Low Profile Positive · 3 pts

What is it?
The chassis presents a very small, squat silhouette to incoming fire, making it a harder target to hit.
BattleTech effect
Enemy units suffer a +1 penalty to their to-hit target numbers when shooting at this 'Mech (ranged attacks only — physical attacks are unaffected).
Alpha Strike
Attacks against this unit take an additional +1 modifier (not applied to physical attacks).
The UrbanMech's short, hunched "garbage-can" build is exactly what this represents. Combined with hiding in city terrain, that +1 to be hit makes the little 'Mech surprisingly survivable for its tonnage and light armor.
Negative Quirks

No / Minimal Arms Negative · −2 pts

What is it?
The 'Mech either has no arms or has arms too stubby/weak to be useful for bracing, catching itself, or manipulating objects.
BattleTech effect
Takes a penalty to Piloting Skill Rolls made to stand up after a fall (it can't push itself upright with its arms). It cannot make physical attacks (punches) or carry/lift items with its arms.
Alpha Strike
Cannot use the External Cargo or Climbing rules.
The UrbanMech's arms are little more than weapon mounts (right arm = AC/10, left arm = small laser), with the lower-arm and hand actuators stripped out. It can't punch and struggles to get back on its feet — so keeping it upright, ideally dug into cover, matters.
Quirks per BattleMech Manual, p. 95 (UrbanMech entry). Rules text summarized from Strategic Operations / BattleMech Manual and the Alpha Strike Companion via Sarna.net. Design Quirks are an optional rule; both players must agree to use them.
