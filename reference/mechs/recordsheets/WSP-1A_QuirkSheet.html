What are Design Quirks?

Design Quirks are an optional BattleTech rules set (from Strategic Operations, collected in the BattleMech Manual) that give an individual 'Mech special positive or negative traits normal construction rules don't capture. Each has a point value — positives cost points, negatives refund them — and they're not counted in Battle Value, so both players must agree to use them. The Wasp is an all-positive design: a cheap, rugged, everywhere-you-look recon 'Mech.

Positive Quirks

Easy to Maintain Positive · 1 pt

What is it?
The 'Mech is built with accessibility and simplicity in mind — panels open easily, parts are standard, and techs don't have to fight the chassis to service it.
Game effect
Grants a bonus to the target-number modifier for Repair and replacement rolls (campaign/maintenance rules). It's faster and more reliable to fix between battles.
A recon 'Mech lives or dies on uptime. The Wasp's simple GM-built frame keeps it in the field with minimal fuss, which is a big reason so many are still running centuries later.

Extended Torso Twist Positive · 3 pts

What is it?
The waist can rotate farther than a normal 'Mech, letting it bring its arm/torso weapons around to the sides without turning its legs.
BattleTech effect
May torso-twist one extra hexside beyond the normal one-hexside limit (up to two each way), widening its firing arc each turn.
Alpha Strike
Gains a full 360° arc of fire (still keeps a Rear arc for taking damage).
Perfect for a jump-and-fade skirmisher: the Wasp can leap into a firing position, then twist to cover threats from several directions before bounding away again.

Ubiquitous Positive · 1 pt

What is it?
The design has been mass-produced for centuries by many manufacturers, so spare parts are everywhere.
Game effect
Easier to find replacement parts. Rolls to locate/obtain spares are simpler because the chassis is so common across the Inner Sphere.
The Wasp is literally the most-produced BattleMech in the setting — tens of thousands built across every Great House. If something breaks, a replacement is almost always nearby.
Quirks per BattleMech Manual, p. 95 (Wasp entry). Rules summarized from Strategic Operations / BattleMech Manual via Sarna.net. Design Quirks are optional; both players must agree to use them. (The primitive WSP-1 prototype instead has Hard to Pilot and Weak Legs.)
